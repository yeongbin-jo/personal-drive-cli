Personal Drive CLI

A personal utility for the account owner's Google Drive, operated with rclone on the owner's devices.

It supports listing and downloading existing files, uploading selected files, and managing files when explicitly requested by the account owner. It is intended for the owner's personal use and does not offer public registration.

Google authorization takes place directly with Google. This website does not accept passwords, authorization codes, tokens, or Drive files.
