Privacy policy

Personal Drive CLI is a personal utility operated by the Google account owner using rclone.

Access and purpose

The utility requests Google Drive permission to list and download existing files, upload files, and rename, move, or delete files when the account owner requests those actions. It may access file metadata, file contents, account identity, and storage quota to perform and verify those operations.

Storage and transfer

OAuth credentials are stored in the owner's local rclone configuration, protected by filesystem permissions. Downloaded files, selected backups, and operation records remain on the owner's devices or at destinations the owner selects. The utility does not operate a separate developer-hosted data collection server.

This static website does not receive Google authorization codes, OAuth tokens, or Google Drive contents. It has no analytics scripts, advertising, or sign-up forms. The website hosting provider may keep its standard access logs.

Sharing and use

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models. Files are transferred only to destinations selected by the account owner for the requested operation. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control, retention, and revocation

The account owner controls local files and backups and can delete them at any time. Google access can be revoked through Google Account connections. Local credentials can also be removed from the rclone configuration. Revoking access does not automatically delete previously downloaded files or backups; the owner controls their removal.
